# Finishing touches

The branch of [`designing-ui`](SKILL.md) for adding visual interest once rank, structure and states hold. Each touch follows the decoration rule for its page type: free on marketing pages, quiet everywhere else.

Done when each touch supports rank or tells similar items apart, the content stays the focus, and every text pair it affects still meets the contrast floor.

- **Start from what is already there.** Swap list bullets for icons that fit the content, enlarge and colour the quotation mark on a testimonial, give links a custom underline, and use the brand colour for selected checkboxes and radios.
- **Accent borders** add colour with no artwork: along the top of a card or the layout, beside an alert or an active nav item, or as a short rule under a headline.
- **Background colour** can emphasise one panel or divide page sections.
- **Gradients** use two hues within about 30° of each other.
- **Patterns** repeat across a background or along one edge, in low contrast against it.
- **Shapes and simple illustrations** sit in one or two chosen spots, in low contrast, clear of the content.
