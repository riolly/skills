# Images and icons

The branch of [`designing-ui`](SKILL.md) for photos, user-uploaded images, screenshots, logos and large icons. Every image and icon has a size it was made for; design around that size.

Done when every image and every icon above 24px on the screen meets the rule for its kind.

- **Text over a photo needs even contrast.** Add an overlay, lower the image's contrast, colourise it, or put a soft glow behind the text.
- **An icon stays at the size it was drawn for**, usually 16 to 24px. To fill a larger space, set it inside a tinted shape, or use an icon drawn for large sizes.
- **A screenshot or logo stays legible at its display size.** Capture at a smaller layout, crop to one region, or redraw a simplified version, as for a favicon.
- **A user upload sits in a fixed container, cropped to cover it.** A faint inner shadow or a semi-transparent inner border keeps it distinct from the background.
- **An avatar falls back to initials** when its image fails to load.
- **Overlap creates layers.** A card may cross the boundary between two backgrounds. Overlapping images each take a border in the background colour.
